GROUPS
Tiny polyester trousers.
On seventy-five rats.
And it destroyed
their sex life.
A surgeon in
Cairo tailored them
himself. Five groups:
polyester, poly-cotton,
pure cotton, pure
wool, and one
group left naked.
Then he waited
a full year,
counting how often
each group mated.
Cotton and wool,
no change at
all. The polyester
rats almost completely
stopped.
The fabric was
generating static
electricity across their
bodies. They were
being quietly electrified
out of the
mood.
Take the trousers
off, six months
later, back to
normal. It won
an Ig Nobel
Prize. Now go
and check what
your underwear is
made of.
