A scientist put
tiny polyester pants
on 75 rats.
He split them
into five groups.
Polyester, poly-cotton,
pure cotton, pure
wool, and one
group left naked.
Then he waited
a full year,
measuring how often
each group mated.
The cotton and
wool rats were
fine. The polyester
rats stopped having
sex almost entirely.
The polyester was
generating static
electricity across their
bodies. The fabric
was literally electrifying
them out of
the mood.
Take the pants
off, and six
months later they
were back to
normal. It was
published in European
Urology.
